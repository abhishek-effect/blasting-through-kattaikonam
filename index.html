HOW TO PLAY

🎮 GAME MODES

🏃 EVADE: ESCAPE MODE

Explore the campus to search for keys that unlock locked laboratory doors. Find the Master Key to unlock the Seminar Hall. Gather your squad inside to obtain the photocopy in hand, then run to the West Wing elevator and escape!

⏱️ EVADE: SURVIVE MODE

All campus doors and the Seminar Hall are unlocked from the start! Work together to survive the relentless Evil Santhomites for 2 to 3 minutes (coordinated countdown). Stay fast, revive fallen friends, and survive until 00:00!

💥 SHOOT SHOOT SHOOT MODE

Retro FPS action! Use your AK-47, Pistol, and Grenades to blast campus enemies. Eliminate 20 enemies to clear the lockdown on the Seminar Hall!

⌨️ CONTROLS & MECHANICS

WASD: Move
Shift: Sprint
Ctrl: Slide (5s cooldown)
V: Rear-View Camera
E: Interact / Revive (Hold 2.5s)
Space: Jump
Left Click: Shoot / Use
1, 2, 3: Switch Weapons
G: Throw Grenade
Esc / P: Pause Menu

GAME OPTIONS

PC Controls

  • WASD: Move | Space: Jump
  • Mouse: Look around (Pointer Lock)
  • Left Click: Fire weapon / Use medkit
  • 1, 2, 3: Switch (Rifle, Pistol, Medkit)
  • G: Throw Grenade (Drops every 3 kills)
  • E: Interact / Elevator / Revive
  • V: Look behind (Rear View)
  • Shift: Sprint | C: Crouch | Ctrl: Slide
  • R: Reload | P / Esc: Pause

Mobile Controls

  • Left Thumbstick: Move
  • Right Half Drag: Aim & Look
  • 👀 REAR Button: Look behind
  • ⬆️ Button: Leap over laser traps
  • Tap Gun / Picture: Switch weapons
  • 💥 FIRE Button: Shoot / Use item
  • 🔄️ Button: Reload weapon
  • 💣 G Button: Throw Grenade
  • USE [E]: Elevator / Revive
  • RUN Button: Toggle sprint
  • CROUCH Button: Crouch / Slide
  • ⏸️ Button: Pause menu
