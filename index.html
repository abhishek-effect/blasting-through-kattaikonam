PC Controls

  • WASD: Move | Space: Jump
  • Mouse: Look around (Pointer Lock)
  • Left Click: Fire weapon / Use medkit
  • 1, 2, 3: Switch (Rifle, Pistol, Medkit)
  • G: Throw Grenade (Drops every 3 kills)
  • E: Interact / Elevator
  • Shift: Sprint | R: Reload

Mobile Controls

  • Left Thumbstick: Move
  • Right Half Drag: Aim & Look
  • JUMP Button: Leap over laser traps
  • Tap HUD Slots 1-3: Switch weapons
  • FIRE Button: Shoot / Use item
  • 💣 G Button: Throw Grenade
  • USE [E]: Elevator interaction
  • RUN Button: Toggle sprint
